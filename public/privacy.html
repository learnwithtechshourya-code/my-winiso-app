1. Privacy Policy

WinISO Hub respects your privacy and keeps user data minimal. All download history, bookmarked ISOs, account preferences, and support request logs are stored locally on your device using Android's private app storage.

  • Local Device Storage: Download records, timestamps, bookmarks, and preferences stay on your device unless you choose to clear them or uninstall the app.
  • Sign-In & Email Requests: Entering an email for Sign-In, Contact Support, or a macOS Pro Access request is used solely to display your local profile or let you send an inquiry via your chosen email client.
  • No Data Selling or Ad Tracking: WinISO Hub does not sell personal information, inject advertising SDKs, or share your browsing activity with third-party data brokers.
  • On-Device SHA-256 Hashing: Selecting a local ISO file for checksum verification processes the file exclusively on your device; files are never uploaded to any server.

2. Official Sources & Third-Party Links

WinISO Hub indexes publicly documented download portals and vendor distribution URLs from official publishers including Microsoft Corporation, Apple Inc., Rufus, Ventoy, Balena, 7-Zip, and Crystal Dew World.

  • When you download an ISO or tool kit, your device connects directly to the vendor's official server or opens the publisher's download portal in your browser.
  • Official product licenses (such as a valid Windows product key or Apple hardware eligibility) remain governed by the respective vendor's license agreement.
  • External websites linked by WinISO Hub are governed by their own privacy policies and terms of service.
